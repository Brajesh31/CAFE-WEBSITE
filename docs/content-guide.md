# ✍️ Content and Asset Guidelines

The current page contains sample café copy, three sample branch addresses, and named testimonials. Do not assume these are real. Verify all business facts with the owner before publishing.

## Menu records
For every item, approve the display name, description, price and currency, ingredients, allergen details, availability, and image rights. Do not invent pricing or dietary claims.

## Branches
Verify each address, city, hours, phone, accessibility details, and publication permission. Do not present sample locations as operating branches.

## Testimonials
Publish only genuine customer statements. Obtain consent for names, photos, and identifying details. Do not fabricate identities, review counts, or ratings. Preserve the meaning when editing.

## Images and fonts
Record creator, source, license, and attribution requirements. Optimize dimensions and formats. Provide alt text. A file in the repository is not automatically free for commercial reuse.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Business facts approved.
- [ ] Menu and prices verified.
- [ ] Locations current.
- [ ] Testimonials genuine and permissioned.
- [ ] Image/font rights checked.
- [ ] Alt text reviewed.
- [ ] Contact details verified.
